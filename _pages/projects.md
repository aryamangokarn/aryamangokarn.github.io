## 1. **Semantic Segmentation for Advanced Land Cover Mapping** 
  *	Developed a novel semantic segmentation approach, by leveraging base models <b>(VGG16 and Resnet50)</b> as the backbone of the segmentation model <b>(U-Net)</b> and achieving an overall classification accuracy of <b>96.63%.</b>
  *	Designed a comprehensive pre-processing pipeline to extract relevant features and generate ground-truth labels, streamlining the training process, resulting in a significant model improvement, achieving a Dice coefficient of <b>0.7.</b>
  * Utilized remote sensing satellite data from Bhuvan Geo-Platform managed by <b>Indian Space Research Organization (ISRO).</b>

## 2. **Covid-19 Radiography using ConvNets** 
  *	Engineered an AI-driven diagnostic tool using ConvNets that analyzed over <b>5,000+</b> chest radiographs, achieving a <b>95.45%</b> accuracy rate in detecting COVID-19 abnormalities, significantly aiding rapid response initiatives.
  *	Led an extensive study comparing state-of-the-art architectures, showcasing <b>VGG-19</b>’s superiority with a <b>4%</b> increase in accuracy over previous methods, positioning our research at the forefront.
  *	Built a dynamic web-app using Streamlit to deliver data-driven insights through immersive, interactive data visualizations.

## 4. **Sankat Mochan Bot - Line Follower Robot with Wireless Communication and Color Detection** 
  *	Designed an FPGA-based robot to autonomously traverse industrial environments, detecting and resolving faults in manufacturing units with 90% accuracy by identifying and implementing corresponding remedies.
  *	Improved fault identification and resolution efficiency in manufacturing plants by developing an FPGA-based robotic system, enabling real-time communication and automated troubleshooting within industrial settings.

## 5. **Virtual Canvas** 
  *	Achieved hands-free digital drawing with 95% accuracy by developing the Virtual Notepad project, utilizing OpenCV to translate air gestures into x, y coordinates for real-time visualization on a digital canvas.
  * The direction of the brush is modified to map the pointer finger onto the screen to measure and record the color of the user’s hand.
  * Enhanced user interaction and creativity in smart environments by implementing vibrant color choices and screenshot features, enabling seamless note-taking and artistic expression in augmented reality settings.
