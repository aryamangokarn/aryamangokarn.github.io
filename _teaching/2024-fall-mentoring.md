Led 4 project teams of 25 students to develop AI-driven projects using LLMs for Accenture, Cohere, Snowflake & Verizon.

Accenture
======
<b>Challenge Summary:</b> <i>Technology News Monitoring and Summarization Engine</i>

A technology news monitoring and summarization engine leverages AI to automatically gather and
analyze large volumes of news data. Natural language processing (NLP) models and/or Large Language Models (LLMs) are
used to identify key topics and extract relevant information, while advanced summarization algorithms distill this
information into concise, easily digestible summaries. Data science and machine learning methods are continuously applied
to optimize these summarization algorithms, improving their accuracy and relevance through techniques such as
reinforcement learning, model fine-tuning, and user feedback integration.

<b>Context / Impact:</b> 
An important part of technology strategy and advisory capabilities at Accenture is accelerating
early-stage startups – helping them find product-market fit by introducing them to the right Accenture clients. In doing
so, Accenture leaders need to keep their finger on the pulse of emerging tech news, identifying trends and companies
on the edge of the innovation lifecycle before they come into the mainstream.

<b>Suggested Approach: </b>
Students teams will identify key sources for news relating to new and emerging technologies
(products, services, companies) with potential applications to large enterprise clients. After identifying sources,
students can use or create web scraping functionality to read the site contents and create tags (using language models
or NLP techniques) to help with identifying, measuring, and understanding trends in technology and how they may
impact client technology landscapes.

Cohere
======
<b>Challenge Summary:</b> <i>AI-Driven Review and Recommendation System</i>

BrandMatch, this challenge aims to analyze retailer reviews of products carried by various stores. The project will involve creating a system that stores these reviews and utilizes LLM techniques to understand sentiment, extract meaningful patterns, and identify key product features appreciated by customers. Subsequently, the system will leverage this analysis to recommend new brands to stores that align with the qualities and preferences identified in the reviews.

<b>Context / Impact:</b> 
This Challenge Project is highly pertinent as it mirrors real-world applications where businesses seek to understand customer sentiment and preferences to better tailor their offerings, hence improving customer satisfaction and business growth. By implementing BrandMatch, our organization can pioneer in providing personalized store recommendations, thus enhancing market competitiveness and driving innovation. The project not only aims to optimize product assortments for stores but also serves as a strategic tool to forecast trends and facilitate data-driven decision-making in inventory management.

<b>Suggested Approach: </b>
Begin with Unsupervised Learning Clustering to analyze review data for patterns and associations, then apply LLM for in-depth sentiment analysis. Finally develop a recommendation system with DL for predictive accuracy.
